THAI FOOD

Thai food has a long history influenced by local traditions, neighboring countries, and international trade. In the past, Thai people used rice, fish, herbs, and vegetables as their main ingredients because these foods were easy to find in Thailand. Chinese traders and immigrants introduced cooking techniques such as stir-frying and noodle-making, while Indian and Persian influences brought spices and new curry styles.
